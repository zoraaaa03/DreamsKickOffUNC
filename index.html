Questions

The stuff people actually ask

Is this gambling?

No. You're buying a keychain for $20 and it's yours no matter what happens next. The number comes with it. If you'd rather not spend anything there's a free way in through Instagram, though those are limited and come with conditions, so DM us and ask.

What if barely anyone buys? Do I still have a shot?

A better one, actually. The shoes get drawn whatever happens, so fewer keychains sold just means fewer numbers in the hat. The odds tile up top is always current.

Can I buy more than one?

Sure. Every $20 is another keychain and another number. Worth knowing though: you can only win one prize, so a second number raises your chance of winning something, not how much you can win.

How do I get my prize if I win?

We message you within an hour of the draw. For the shoes you tell us which pair you want and your size, US 4 through 13, order that night, and post the confirmation on this page. If your size is gone everywhere you pick: wait for a restock, take another University Blue colorway, or take the cash instead. Handoff is on campus, or we'll ship it if you've already left town.

Who's holding the money?

Our team's Venmo. At the end of the project we hand our numbers to the course TA, and the net profit has to match what's actually sitting in that account. That's a course requirement, not something we came up with.

Are you affiliated with Nike or UNC Athletics?

No. We're students in a class. Nike, Apple, Marshall and UNC Athletics have nothing to do with this and haven't endorsed it. Everything in the pool gets bought at regular retail, same as anyone else would.

How to enter

Buy a keychain for $20. Each keychain comes with one numbered entry, issued once payment has been received. There is no limit on how many you may buy, but see "One prize per person" below.

The prize pool

The shoes are awarded regardless of how many entries are issued, and that winner chooses between the two pairs shown. Each further prize is added to the pool once the stated number of entries has been reached, to a maximum of five prizes.

    All prizes are new and bought at ordinary retail. Prizes are ordered after the drawing so that a winner can state their own size or colour where that applies.

    Odds of winning

    Odds depend on the number of entries issued and equal the number of prizes awarded divided by the total number of entries. At each hundredth entry the ratio is one in one hundred. The current figure is shown at the top of this page and changes as entries are issued.

    Substitution

    If a winner's size, colour or a specific item cannot be obtained, the winner may choose to wait for it to come back in stock, accept a comparable alternative, or take the cash equivalent of the prize instead. No other substitution is offered and prizes are not transferable before they are handed over.

    One prize per person

    No entrant may win more than one prize. If an entry belonging to someone who has already won is drawn again, that draw is void and a replacement entry is drawn by the same method, repeating as needed until the prize goes to a different person.

    How the winner is drawn

    Before any entry was issued, the sponsor generated a random seed and published its SHA-256 hash on this page. After entries close, the seed itself is published. Anyone can hash the published seed and confirm it matches the hash that was posted before selling began.

    Winning numbers are then derived from that seed alone. For i = 1, 2, 3 and so on, take SHA-256(seed + ":" + total entries + ":" + i), read the digest as an integer, take it modulo the total number of entries, and add one. Skip any number already drawn, and any number belonging to someone who has already won, until every prize has a winner.

    The result is fixed the moment entries close, and any person can reproduce it. The drawing is also carried out live on Instagram.

    Winner notification

    Each winner is notified by direct message, or at the contact given when they entered, within 24 hours of the drawing. Winning numbers are posted on this page. If a winner cannot be reached within 72 hours, that draw is void and a replacement is drawn by the same method.

    Prizes are handed over on the UNC campus, or shipped within the United States at the sponsor's cost if the winner has left the area. Order confirmations are posted on this page within 48 hours of the drawing.
